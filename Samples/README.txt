AUREON sample library

No piano recordings are included in this prototype.

Only add recordings that you created yourself or are explicitly licensed to use. Do not copy samples from Keyzone Classic or another commercial instrument.

The planned sample engine will support:
- MIDI note/root-note mapping
- Multiple velocity layers
- Optional round-robin variations
- Release samples and sustain behavior
- Loop points

A sample mapping specification must be finalized before building a production library.
